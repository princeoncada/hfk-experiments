## Design Evolution

### Level 2 Upgrades — Visual Polish `[MILESTONE-LOCKED: M1]`
Apply to all worksheets once M1 (First Signal) is hit. These are Canva-only changes.

- **Rounded section cards** — each activity section inside a rounded rectangle with soft fill (`#FFF9D6` light yellow or `#F5F5F5` light gray)
- **Numbered circle badges** — filled circle shape with number on top, replaces plain "1." header
- **"I can…" intent banner** — thin strip below the header image row: *"I can [skill]!"* — sets learning goal, popular with parents
- **Bordered answer boxes** — actual drawn boxes or lined fields instead of underscores
- **Star reward strip** — bottom of page: 3–5 empty star outlines with caption *"Color a star when you finish each activity!"* — highly shareable/saveable
- **Framed images** — illustrations inside circle or rounded-square frames, not floating

### Level 3 Upgrades — Brand Identity `[MILESTONE-LOCKED: M2]`
Introduce after baseline is established and we know what visual style wins.

- **HFK mascot character** — a consistent cartoon kid or animal that appears across all worksheets. Builds brand recognition. Needs a Recraft session dedicated to character design and locking the style.
- **Color-coded activity types** — trace activities always blue-accented, fill-in always green, match always yellow. Builds visual language parents recognize.
- **DepEd curriculum badge** — small "K-12 Aligned" or "DepEd Grade X" stamp in corner. Filipino parents specifically respond to curriculum alignment signals.
- **Full illustrated header scenes** — for Style B (Storybook) experiments, the header becomes a full-width illustrated scene, not just icons in a strip.

### Level 4 Upgrades — Product Polish `[MILESTONE-LOCKED: M3]`
Unlock after a winner is confirmed. At this point we're building a product, not just testing.

- **Seasonal and holiday variants** — Christmas, Easter, Valentine's, back-to-school versions of winning combinations
- **Themed worksheet bundles** — 5–10 page PDFs around a winner combination
- **Answer key pages** — Page 2 of every worksheet is a parent answer key + extension activity suggestion
- **Consistent character story** — the HFK mascot has a name and appears in every post doing the same activity as the worksheet

## Content Expansion

### New Grade Ranges `[IDEA]`
Currently targeting Grade 1. Future content tiers to test:
- Preschool / Kinder (ages 4–5) — tracing, colors, shapes, counting to 10
- Grade 2–3 — multi-step math, reading comprehension, grammar
- Mixed (K-3 bundle) — one worksheet, three difficulty levels on separate pages

### New Content Types `[IDEA]`
Additions to the variable matrix for Sprint 2+:
- **Coloring + phonics hybrid** — B&W line art worksheet where coloring IS the activity (color the short-A words red, long-A words blue)
- **Cut-and-paste** — kids cut out word/image cards and paste them in the right places. High engagement for younger kids.
- **Journal prompt** — one writing prompt with illustrated border. Tests parent tip / creative writing audience.
- **Bilingual (English + Filipino)** — worksheet with English instructions and Filipino labels. Taglish caption. Tests bilingual segment.

### DepEd Alignment `[PLANNED]` `[MILESTONE-LOCKED: M2]`
Tag experiments with the specific DepEd learning competency they address.
Mention competency code in caption. Test whether "DepEd-aligned" hook drives more saves from PH parents.
